# Surface brief: circloo web (landing + app shell)

**Scope**: public landing `/` (Persuade); sign-in, welcome, home, settings, icebreakers and not-found (Operate, inheriting the same world). Replaces the fridge-door world (issue #60).
**Audience**: families spread across languages, friend groups, small teams. Job: understand circloo in seconds, play one icebreaker, start a circle. In the app: check in, run an icebreaker round, and ask circloo for things in plain words.
**Proof/content**: real seed sparks (content/fun); example check-ins and boards labelled as examples; integrations honestly "coming soon". No testimonials, logos or metrics.
**Constraints**: en/fr/ar(RTL)/es, strict CSP (no inline style/script, self-hosted fonts), WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion, light and night themes.
**Memorable moment**: the visitor taps the water and a ring of ripples spreads: the circloo mark coming alive. Dealing a new icebreaker drops it into the pool with the same ring.
**Software 3.0**: the Ask bar (Ctrl/⌘ K) on every app screen turns plain words into a plan the person checks before it runs. AI output always lands as cards to keep or dismiss, labelled "Suggested by AI", and an autonomy setting (Off / Suggest) governs inline suggestions.
**Unresolved**: no photography yet; every illustration is drawn in code or SVG.

## Direction contract

THESIS: circloo is the neighbourhood lido for your circle: the shared water where a family or a team meets every day. Check-ins are the chalkboard by the gate, boards are swim lanes, icebreakers are the diving board. It refuses the SaaS hero with three feature cards, and it refuses the old skeuomorphic fridge. Its world is flat, bright and contemporary, built from full-strength fields.

OWN-WORLD: pool aqua drenches the first viewport as one flat field. Deep-end navy is the ink and the night theme. Cabin-door yellow carries the primary action, and lane-rope coral marks "now" and turns. Tile white is the deck, with a quiet grout grid only where content is laid out. Cards are white tiles with crisp 16px corners and soft, offset depth (never a hard block). Lane ropes (chains of floats) are the dividers and the turn indicator. Kufam 800 is painted depth-marker signage for display and numerals. Readex Pro is the text face. The ring mark is two open rings.

STORY: the visitor sees the water and reads "Jump in. Your people are here." They understand the three jobs as three lanes (feel, work, have fun) and play a live icebreaker floating on the water. They see the coming-soon integrations as a row of cabin doors, then tap the yellow "Start your circle".

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed aqua water with canvas caustics. On top, a header with the ring-mark lockup at inline-start, and the language switch plus "Sign in" at inline-end. Inline-start 55%: the Kufam headline at about 6rem in navy, the lead, the four-language line, the yellow CTA and a quiet link. Inline-end 45%: a white tile floating with a gentle bob that holds the live icebreaker and its "Deal another" action. Under it sits the navy chalkboard "Today at the pool" with four example check-ins. The lane-rope water line closes the field.

FORM: grounded candidate 7 of 7 (municipal lido), seed key 03004b4f. Raises:
- From the cutting bench (declined): state is a mark, never a hue alone. Now, kept and done each carry a drawn mark.
- From datamatics (declined): numerals are a system. Every count, timer and allowance is set in Kufam tabular figures as a depth marker.
- From the session sleeve (declined): full-strength flat fields own whole regions, never tints of tints.
- From tensegrity (declined): the structure is visible. Lane ropes are the page grid, and each section sits in its lane.
- Brine calendar and sampler: declined, nothing kept.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
